Welcome to MC Beats

MC Beats is a live procedural synth with drums, bass, chords, lead. All sound is synthesized from oscillators and procedural noise; there are no samples.

There are three ways to interact with MC Beats.

1. Play it yourself

Click any pad, key, or keyboard shortcut to play the focused instrument. Click another instrument to put it into focus. Choose presets and adjust the controls or envelope settings. Use Record, or press R, to capture a four-bar take on the active deck. Play the active deck with the Play/Pause button or the Space bar. Switch decks with the large slider.

2. Get your agent to play

In an MCP-enabled browser, ask your agent to create a groove in the active deck, or prepare one in the inactive deck. Ask it to transfer between decks, change sound presets, or turn instruments on and off. It can also solo over the groove for extra sparkle.

3. Play with your agent

Play live while your agent builds or changes material in the background. Try requests such as “Add a bass pattern to Deck B at the next bar” or “Create a lead solo over the next four bars.” It can see what you are playing and join in at the right time and on the right beat.
